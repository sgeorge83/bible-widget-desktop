# Privacy Policy — Bible Widget Desktop

**Effective date:** August 20, 2026  
**App:** Bible Widget — Verse of the Day (Windows)  
**Publisher:** WordOnAir Labs  
**Homepage:** https://wordonair.com

Bible Widget Desktop is a floating Windows widget that shows a daily ESV Bible verse and a short insight. It works without accounts, ads, or unnecessary personal data collection.

## 1. Information we collect

The app does **not** require a Microsoft account and does not ask for your name, email, phone number, or contacts.

When the widget refreshes, it requests verse content over the internet:

- Standard HTTPS requests are made to our verse service (`https://bible-widget-backend.vercel.app/api/morning`).
- Technical connection details typical of any HTTPS request (such as IP address and user-agent) may be processed by the hosting provider for operation and security.
- The app stores the last verse and window position locally on your PC so the widget can still display content offline and remember where you placed it. This cache stays on your device.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use advertising SDKs or advertising IDs.

## 2. How information is used

- To deliver the daily verse, reference, and Verse Insight.
- To keep a local cache of the last verse and window layout.
- To operate and secure the verse backend service.

## 3. Permissions / capabilities

- **Internet (Client)** — to fetch today's verse when online.

The app does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos, or files outside its own local settings folder.

## 4. Third-party services

Verse content is provided through our backend, which may obtain scripture text from the ESV API and related public verse-of-the-day sources, and may use constrained AI processing to produce a short simplified meaning.

Scripture quotations are from the ESV® Bible (The Holy Bible, English Standard Version®), copyright © Crossway.

## 5. Data retention

Local cache remains on your PC until you delete `%USERPROFILE%\.bible-widget-desktop` or uninstall the app. Server-side request logs (if any) follow the hosting provider’s normal security practices.

## 6. Children’s privacy

Bible Widget is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app has no ads.

## 7. Your choices

- Close or uninstall the app at any time.
- Delete the local cache folder `%USERPROFILE%\.bible-widget-desktop`.

## 8. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The effective date at the top will change when we do.
