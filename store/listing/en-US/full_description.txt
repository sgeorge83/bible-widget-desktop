Bible Widget places today’s English Standard Version (ESV) verse on your Windows desktop as a floating, always-on-top widget — the same daily WordOnAir experience you know from Android.

Features
• Verse of the Day with scripture text and reference
• Short Verse Insight / simplified meaning
• Transparent light-navy glass so your wallpaper shows through
• Drag to move, resize from the edges, pin on top
• Updates each morning (Asia/Dubai schedule) and when the API refreshes
• Works offline with the last cached verse
• Tap WORDONAIR LABS to visit wordonair.com
